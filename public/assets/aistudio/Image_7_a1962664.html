Campus Admissions Help

Campus Visit & Admissions FAQ

Common questions answered about traveling to our physical campuses, installment plans, and scholarship quotas.

Do I need to bring my own laptop to the campus labs? expand_more

No. All NexSkill labs at Arfa Tower, Garden Town, Karachi, and Mian Channu are fully equipped with dedicated workstations featuring fast processors, dual screens, and pre-installed developer runtimes. Students are welcome to bring personal laptops if they prefer personal workspace persistence.

Are PSDF or NAVTTC government-sponsored seats available at all branches? expand_more

Yes! Under national workforce programs, subsidized and 100% free seats funded by NAVTTC and PSDF are periodically opened at our campuses. Inquire directly during your physical campus visit or check the scholarship checkbox on the form to check cohort enrollment quotas.

Can I switch between On-Campus and Online modes if my schedule shifts? expand_more

NexSkill offers hybrid flexibility. If you relocate or require flexibility during exams or work shifts, you can request an easy mode transition via the NexSkill LMS App without forfeiting lecture credits or instructor mentoring access.

What parking and transit options exist at the Arfa Tower & Garden Town campuses? expand_more

Arfa Software Technology Park has direct, covered access to the Lahore Metro Bus Line (Shahnoor / Chungi Amar Sidhu corridor) as well as secure multi-story vehicle parking. Garden Town Campus provides street and dedicated lot parking adjacent to Barkat Market commercial area.
