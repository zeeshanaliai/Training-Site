## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a premium, authoritative, and outcome-oriented identity for Pakistan's premier IT and vocational training academy. The system combines the rigorous credibility of premier enterprise software with the direct engagement and conversion mechanics of global learning powerhouses like Coursera, Springboard, and Maven.

### Brand Personality
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Anchored in career outcomes, industry accreditations (NAVTTC, PBTE), and verified job placement metrics.
- **Dynamic & Action-Driven:** High-contrast focus states, clear directional CTAs, and disciplined visual hierarchies that guide prospects from discovery to enrollment.
- **Enterprise-Grade Modern:** Crisp white working surfaces, surgical borders, and deep slate navy typography that replaces casual boot-camp chaos with executive polish.

### Design Movement
The system balances **Corporate Modernism** with purposeful **Data-Dense Utility**:
- Canvas clarity through generous whitespace and strict baseline modularity.
- Restrained application of vibrant crimson (#DC2626) to reserve urgency strictly for key conversions, critical badges, and active learning indicators.
- Technical authority powered by deep slate containers, emerald metrics for outcome validation, and structured metadata cards.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around visual contrast, institutional legitimacy, and unambiguous conversion cues. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#DC2626` - Nex Crimson):** The primary brand anchor. Used exclusively for primary buttons, application triggers, deadline badges, active tab markers, and primary brand accents. Must not be used for generic backgrounds or low-priority highlights.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` - Deep Tech Slate):** The typographic anchor and high-impact ground. Powers primary headlines, dark-mode callout shells, hero header navigation bars, and institutional trust footers.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Metric Emerald):** Dedicated to verified outcomes: "96% Placement Rate", "NAVTTC Certified", "Batches Enrolling", and positive status tags.
- **Neutral Foundations (`#64748B` - Slate Core):** Scaled across structural neutrals:
  - Surface Canvas: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subtle Section Ground: `#F8FAFC`
  - Border & Dividers: `#E2E8F0`
  - Secondary Text / Subheaders: `#475569`
  - Muted Form Placeholders & Labels: `#94A3B8`

### Color Balance & Surface Hierarchy
1. 70% Neutral canvas (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`) to preserve optical breathing room across course-heavy catalogs.
2. 20% Deep Slate (`#0F172A` and `#1E293B`) to establish structural hierarchy in navigation, course cards, and typography.
3. 10% High-intent accents (`#DC2626` for action; `#10B981` for credibility).

## Typography

The type system relies on **Inter** to deliver industrial readability across dense educational curriculums, pricing tables, and marketing pages.

### Typographic Principles
- **Tracking Discipline:** Tighter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on large headlines prevents sprawling and instills a unified, modern tech aesthetic. Positive tracking (`0.04em`) is reserved exclusively for micro-labels and status indicators to enhance scannability.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Line heights follow an intentional proportional ratio (1.25x for display, 1.5x–1.6x for continuous body copy) to ensure high retention when reading comprehensive module outlines.
- **Hierarchical Anchoring:** Course titles and stats receive substantial weight variations (`700` and `800`), separating content structures clearly from metadata strings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum canvas constraint of `1280px` for optimal reading comfort.

### Breakpoints & Columns
- **Desktop (1024px and up):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) margins. Course grids execute in 3-column arrangements; partner proof bars run horizontally without wrap.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `1.5rem` margins. Course cards reflow into 2 columns; admission sidebars dock below primary descriptions.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) outer margins. Cards collapse into single-column vertical stacks.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Dense, structured UI controls: Micro spacing tokens (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) bind label tags, ratings, and course duration icons directly to their subjects.
- Section rhythm: Large components (e.g., Curriculum accordions, Faculty showcases) maintain `space-xl` separation to ensure learning tracks do not blend together visually.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp, structured layers rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing technical sophistication.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background surfaces (`#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`) with zero elevation. Defined by subtle structural borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Subtle ambient depth for course cards and filter toolbars:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
  Paired with an intentional border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Engagement):** Course cards and interactive selectors elevate slightly on hover to signal affordance:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
  Border shifts to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Quick Admissions Drawers):** Floating overlays:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06);`
- **Level 4 (Sticky Lead Bar / Header Nav):** Crisp glassmorphic backing over pure white canvas:
  `backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92); border-bottom: 1px solid #E2E8F0;`

## Shapes

The design system implements a clean `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px base radius), communicating precision, software fluency, and approachability.

### Radius Assignments
- **Controls & Form Elements (8px / `0.5rem`):** Applied uniformly to inputs, select dropdowns, primary buttons, and tabs.
- **Containers & Course Cards (16px / `1rem` via `rounded-lg`):** Delivers clean separation for cards, certificate preview panels, and curriculum accordions.
- **Large Hero Badges & Modals (24px / `1.5rem` via `rounded-xl`):** Applied to spotlight banners, promotional bundles, and high-impact placement stats boards.
- **Pill Exception (Full Round / 9999px):** Applied strictly to micro-status indicators (e.g., "Trending", "Govt Approved", "Weekend Batch") to maintain instant visual categorization distinct from interactive buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Admissions / Enroll Now):** Fill `#DC2626`, typography `label-md` in `#FFFFFF`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`, corner radius `0.5rem`. Hover: `#B91C1C`. Focused: Ring of `3px` in `#FECACA`.
- **Secondary Action (Download Syllabus / View Outline):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, typography `label-md` in `#0F172A`. Hover: Background `#F8FAFC`, border `#94A3B8`.
- **High-Contrast Dark (Corporate Training / Portal Login):** Fill `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: `#1E293B`.

### Course Cards
- **Structure:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, rounded `1rem`, internal padding `1.25rem`.
- **Header:** Course banner image (16:9 ratio, rounded `0.5rem`) with top-left floating badge (e.g., "AI & Emerging Tech").
- **Body:**
  - Course title: `headline-sm` in `#0F172A`.
  - Metadata row: Duration icon, batch schedule, and lecture mode (On-Campus / Online) set in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).
  - Rating & Review strip: Star glyph in `#F59E0B`, numeric score (`4.9/5`), student enrollment count.
- **Footer:** Separated by a `1px` subtle divider (`#F1F5F9`). Left: Price / Installment plan. Right: Primary CTA button ("Enroll Now").

### Badges & Chips
- **Accreditation Chip (NAVTTC / PBTE):** Emerald tint background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`, rounded `9999px`, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`.
- **Urgency Chip (Limited Seats / Next Batch):** Crimson tint background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.

### Form Controls & Lead Inquiries
- **Text Inputs & Selects:** Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `body-md` in `#0F172A`, corner radius `0.5rem`. Active focus: Border `#DC2626` with subtle outline ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Size `18px`, border `1.5px solid #94A3B8`, active fill `#DC2626` with white checkmark.

### Metric Callouts & Social Proof

- **Partner Badges:** Grayscale SVG logos scaled to max height `28px`, opacity `0.75`, transitioning to `1.0` on hover.
